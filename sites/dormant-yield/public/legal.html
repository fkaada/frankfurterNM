      How we are paid

      We are an independent referrer. If a company applies to micro1 through a link on this site and a qualifying deal completes, micro1 may pay us a referral fee. Companies pay us nothing, and our fee does not come out of any payment to the company. Links that can earn us a fee are labelled "Referral link" and marked as sponsored.

      No affiliation

      We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for micro1. We are not its staff, agents or representatives and cannot make commitments on its behalf. micro1 alone decides who qualifies and on what terms. micro1 is a trade mark of its owner and is used here only to identify the programme.

      Not advice

      This site gives general information about how business records may be licensed. It is not legal, tax, insolvency or data protection advice. Rules depend on the facts and the jurisdiction. Take advice before you agree anything.

      Accuracy

      We describe partner programmes as their own public pages describe them and date our checks. Programmes change without notice. Check the partner's current terms before relying on anything here. If you spot something out of date, email contact@nordicmartech.com and we will correct it.

      Records

      Never send company records to us. We do not receive, store, value or process them.
