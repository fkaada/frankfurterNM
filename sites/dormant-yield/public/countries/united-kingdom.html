      United Kingdom · main focus

      Closed and closing companies in United Kingdom: who can license the records

      Records only become an asset for creditors when someone with authority can license them. Here is how that works in United Kingdom, in plain terms.

      Who usually decides: Administrators and liquidators (licensed insolvency practitioners).

      Who usually decides in United KingdomAdministrators and liquidators (licensed insolvency practitioners)
      Rights guide · main focus

      While the company is in a formal process

      • In administration or liquidation, the officeholder controls the company's assets and decides whether to license its records.
      • In a members' voluntary liquidation, the liquidator appointed by the members holds that authority.

      After dissolution or deregistration

      • Once a company is dissolved, its remaining property generally passes to the Crown as bona vacantia (Companies Act 2006, s.1012).
      • Nobody can usually license those records unless the company is first restored to the register.

      Personal data

      • Personal data stays covered by UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 whoever holds it.
      • Employee and customer data must be removed or properly de-identified before anything is shared.

      Where to check status

      Companies House shows status, dissolution dates and officeholder appointments; The Gazette publishes insolvency notices.
