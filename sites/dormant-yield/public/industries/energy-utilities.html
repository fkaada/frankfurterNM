      Energy retail and utilities: records from closed and closing companies

      If you control the assets of an energy retail and utilities company in administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, its operational records may be licensable. Here is what usually exists, what needs care and how to check fit.

      What usually exists

      • Customer service tickets
      • Metering and billing procedures
      • Field operations records

      What needs care

      • Customer and payment data must be excluded
      • Regulatory obligations may apply

      Employee and HR records always need the most care, whatever the industry.
