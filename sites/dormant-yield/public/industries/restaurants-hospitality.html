    Restaurants and hospitality groups: records from closed and closing companies

    If you control the assets of a restaurants and hospitality groups company in administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, its operational records may be licensable. Here is what usually exists, what needs care and how to check fit.

    What usually exists

    • Operations manuals and training guides
    • Guest feedback and booking support
    • Supplier records

    What needs care

    • Guest personal data must be de-identified
    • Franchise agreements may restrict sharing

    Employee and HR records always need the most care, whatever the industry.
