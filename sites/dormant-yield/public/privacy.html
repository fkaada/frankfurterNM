This website

  • No cookies and no tracking. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. Fonts and images are served from our own domain.

When you email us

We use your name, email address and what you write to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in answering enquiries. We keep correspondence for as long as the conversation is relevant and no longer than two years after it ends.

Outreach to officeholders

We may email administrators, liquidators and bankruptcy trustees at their business addresses about data from estates they manage. We find them in public sources such as The Gazette and court filings, never behind log-ins. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. Every message says we may receive a referral fee and tells you how to opt out. Reply "stop" and we will not contact you again. Contact data we do not use is deleted after 90 days.

What we never do

  • We never receive, see or store the records of the companies we write about.
  • We never sell or share your personal data with micro1 or anyone else. If you apply to micro1, you do that directly with them under their own privacy notice.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to our use of it, or ask us to restrict it. Email contact@nordicmartech.com. You can also complain to Datatilsynet (Norway) or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.
