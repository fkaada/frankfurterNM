      For closed and closing companies · UK, US, Canada and Australia first

      A closed company's records can be an extra asset for creditors.

      AI developers license real business records: support tickets, project histories, internal documents, team chat. If you control the assets of a company in administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, this site explains how licensing works, what to check first and where to apply.

      Step 1 · Who this is for

      Whoever now controls the assets

      A closed company cannot sign anything itself. The person now legally in charge of its assets has to. In insolvency that person is called the officeholder. Guide for officeholders →

      • Administrators and liquidatorsUK licensed insolvency practitioners selling a company's assets to pay its creditors.
      • Bankruptcy trustees and assigneesUS trustees in Chapter 7 cases, and assignees in an assignment for the benefit of creditors, a state-law alternative to bankruptcy.
      • Licensed insolvency trustees and receiversCanadian professionals who sell a company's property for its creditors.
      • Liquidators and voluntary administratorsAustralian registered liquidators and administrators in control of company property.
      • Buyers of the business assetsIf you bought the business or its assets, for example in a pre-pack, check whether the sale covered the records.
      • Former directorsOnce a company is in insolvency, directors usually lose control of its assets. Pass this page to the officeholder.
      Step 2 · What is licensed

      How the work was done, with the people taken out

      AI developers want to learn how real businesses solved problems: how a ticket got fixed, how a project ran. They do not want to know who anyone was. A licence gives permission to use the records on agreed terms; nothing is sold outright.

      Step 5 · Before you talk to a buyer

      Four checks before you talk to any buyer

      Most records from closed companies are never licensed because nobody can show they are allowed to license them. Settle these first, or work through the readiness checklist.

      Authority

      Who controls the company's assets today, and can they sign a licence? Get this in writing.

      Records quality

      Are the records complete, readable and still accessible? Lapsed software subscriptions often mean lost exports.

      Coverage

      Which years, systems and data types exist, and are they in English?

      Permissions

      Customer contracts, confidentiality clauses, software terms and employee data can each limit what may be shared.

      United Kingdom

      • In administration or liquidation, the officeholder controls the company's assets and decides whether to license its records.
      • Once a company is dissolved, its remaining property generally passes to the Crown as bona vacantia (Companies Act 2006, s.1012).
      Full United Kingdom guide →

      United States

      • In bankruptcy, the trustee (or the debtor in possession) sells estate assets, and sales outside the ordinary course need court approval (11 U.S.C. §363).
      • Outside bankruptcy, state law on dissolved corporations decides who can still act for the company and for how long.
      Full United States guide →

      Canada

      • In bankruptcy, the licensed insolvency trustee holds and realises the company's property (Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act).
      • When a federal corporation is dissolved, property it had not disposed of generally vests in the Crown (Canada Business Corporations Act, s.228). Provincial laws are similar.
      Full Canada guide →

      Australia

      • In liquidation or voluntary administration, the liquidator or administrator controls the company's property (Corporations Act 2001).
      • When a company is deregistered, its remaining property vests in ASIC (Corporations Act 2001, s.601AD).
      Full Australia guide →

      FAQ

      Questions people actually ask

      Do I pay anything?

      No. We charge nothing. Any referral fee is paid by micro1 and is disclosed on this page.

      Will you see our records?

      No. Records go only to the buyer, under its own agreement, after you have signed. Never send records to us or to anyone who asks for them before a contract.

      The company was struck off years ago. Can I still do this?

      In the UK, a dissolved company's property generally passes to the Crown until the company is restored to the register, so nobody can usually license its records directly. Restoration is possible in some cases; speak to a solicitor first. In the US it depends on state law and whether the company went through bankruptcy.

      What about employee data?

      Employee and HR records are the most sensitive part of company data. Expect them to be excluded or properly de-identified, and check whether consent or notice is needed before anything is shared.

      How long does it take?

      Timing is set by micro1's agreement. Check micro1's current terms. Confirming who holds the rights can take time too.

      I was a director, not the officeholder. Can I apply?

      Usually not. While a company is in administration, liquidation or bankruptcy, the officeholder controls its assets. Pass this page to them.
