      What a Chapter 7 trustee should know about operational records

      A Chapter 7 trustee collects and realises estate property. Operational records can be part of it. A short guide to spotting, preserving and assessing them.

      Dormant Yield editorial · 15 November 2026 · General information, not legal advice

      United StatesWhat a Chapter 7 trustee should know about operational recordsDormant Yield · Insights · 15 November 2026

      In Chapter 7, the trustee's job is to collect and reduce to money the property of the estate (11 U.S.C. §704). Equipment, receivables and intellectual property are familiar. Operational records are less so, but they can be a possible extra asset for creditors.

      Spot them early

      The biggest risk is time. Cloud tools stop working when invoices go unpaid, and exports disappear with them. Early in the case, list the systems the company used: ticketing, project tools, shared drives, CRM and Slack or Teams.

      Preserve before you assess

      • Secure admin access and export each system while you still can.
      • Store exports away from former employees' accounts.
      • Note what personal data each system holds.
