Rights check

Four checks before you talk to any buyer

Most dormant data never gets licensed because nobody can show they are allowed to license it. Settle these first, or work through the readiness checklist.

Authority

Who controls the company's assets today, and can they sign a licence? Get this in writing.

Records quality

Are the records complete, readable and still accessible? Lapsed software subscriptions often mean lost exports.

Coverage

Which years, systems and data types exist, and are they in English?

Permissions

Customer contracts, confidentiality clauses, software terms and employee data can each limit what may be shared.

What is licensed

Operational records, with people taken out

Usually of interest

  • Support tickets and how they were resolved
  • Project and task histories
  • Internal documents, wikis and process guides
  • CRM and sales pipeline activity
  • Slack or Teams threads and email about the work

Handle with care or leave out

  • Employee and HR records: the most sensitive part of any company's data
  • Customer personal data, unless properly de-identified
  • Payment card and bank account data
  • Health, children's or biometric data
  • Privileged or client-confidential material
