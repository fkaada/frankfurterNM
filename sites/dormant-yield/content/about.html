About

An independent referrer, and nothing more

Dormant Yield helps the people who control closed and closing companies see whether the records left behind could be licensed, and points them to micro1's defunct-company track when it fits.

Who runs it

Kaada Nordic MarTech
Org. no. 936 289 835 (Norway)
contact@nordicmartech.com

How we are paid

If a company applies to micro1 through our link and a qualifying deal completes, micro1 may pay us a referral fee. Companies pay us nothing, and our fee does not come out of any payment to the company. Every link that can earn us a fee is labelled "Referral link".

What we never do

  • We never see, buy, hold, process or value company records.
  • We never present ourselves as micro1 staff, agents or representatives, and we cannot make commitments for micro1.
  • We never quote what a dataset might be worth. micro1 decides what qualifies and on what terms.
  • We never set cookies or track you on this site.
