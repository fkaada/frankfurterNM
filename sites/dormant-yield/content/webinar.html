Company records as an extra asset for creditors

A 30-minute guide for administrators, liquidators, trustees and receivers in the UK, the US, Canada and Australia. Watch it when it suits you. It covers who can act, which records can qualify, the four checks to run first and how micro1's programme works.

Automated webinar. The presenter's voice and the answers to your questions are AI-generated from the webinar's own content. There is no live audience, and nothing in it is legal, tax or insolvency advice.

What the 30 minutes cover

  1. Why it matters (4 min). Operational records are often deleted when a company closes. Some AI developers now license de-identified operational records, so they can be one more asset to consider.
  2. Who can act (6 min). The officeholder, a restored company or a documented asset buyer in the UK; the trustee in the US; the licensed insolvency trustee in Canada; and why deregistration matters in Australia.
  3. What can qualify (7 min). Tickets, documents, workflows and process records. Customer, vendor and contact lists are out. Personal data is removed or de-identified, and employee data needs advice first.
  4. Four checks (6 min). Authority, records quality, coverage and permissions, then the five-question fit check.
  5. How micro1's programme works (4 min). micro1 reviews and decides; a contract sets scope, price and handling. Timing is set by micro1's agreement. Check micro1's current terms.
  6. Your next step (3 min). Run the fit check, apply directly or ask us a question.

Who it is for

  • Insolvency practitioners and their teams handling companies with 30 or more staff at their peak.
  • Buyers of business assets whose sale agreement covers the records.
  • Not for former directors of a dissolved company acting alone: the authority sits elsewhere, and the webinar explains where.
