In a Canadian bankruptcy under the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act, a licensed insolvency trustee holds and realises the company's property for creditors. In a CCAA restructuring the company usually stays in charge under a court-appointed monitor, and sales outside the ordinary course need court approval (Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act, s.36). A court-appointed receiver usually needs the court to approve a sale too (Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act, s.243).

Records as property

Operational records can be part of that property. Licensed to an AI developer with personal data removed, they can be a possible extra asset for creditors.

Personal information

PIPEDA, and provincial laws in Quebec, British Columbia and Alberta, continue to apply to personal information in the records. PIPEDA allows sharing in a business transaction without consent only under a written agreement, and not where the main purpose of the deal is the personal information itself (s.7.2). Take advice before any sample leaves your hands, and leave employee records out.

After dissolution

When a federal corporation is dissolved, property it had not disposed of generally vests in the Crown (Canada Business Corporations Act, s.228). Provincial laws are similar. Revival may be needed before anything can be licensed.
