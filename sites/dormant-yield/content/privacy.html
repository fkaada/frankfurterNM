Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who we are

Dormant Yield is run by Kaada Nordic MarTech, a Norwegian sole proprietorship (org. no. 936 289 835). We are the controller for the personal data described here. Contact: contact@nordicmartech.com.

This website

  • No cookies and no tracking. The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. Fonts and images are served from our own domain.
  • The fit check runs in your browser. Your answers are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Server logs. Our host, Vercel Inc., processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Vercel acts as our processor under its data processing agreement, which includes safeguards for transfers outside the EEA and UK.
  • If we add analytics, it will be cookieless and aggregate, and we will update this notice first.

When you email us

We use your name, email address and what you write to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and the UK GDPR). We keep correspondence for as long as the conversation is relevant and no longer than two years after it ends.

Outreach to officeholders

We may email administrators, liquidators and bankruptcy trustees at their business addresses about data from estates they manage. We find them in public sources such as The Gazette and court filings, never behind log-ins. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in telling officeholders about an option for the estates they manage (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and the UK GDPR). You can object at any time. Every message says we may receive a referral fee and tells you how to opt out. Reply "stop" and we will not contact you again. Contact data we do not use is deleted after 90 days.

What we never do

  • We never receive, see or store the records of the companies we write about.
  • We never sell or share your personal data with micro1 or anyone else. If you apply to micro1, you do that directly with them under their own privacy notice.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to our use of it, or ask us to restrict it. Email contact@nordicmartech.com. You can also complain to Datatilsynet (Norway) or, in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.
